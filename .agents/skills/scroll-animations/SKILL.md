# Scroll & Micro-Animations Skill

## 1. Principles
- **Subtle Lift**: Cards lift upwards (`transform: translateY(-2px to -4px)`) on hover with refined box-shadow transitions.
- **Smooth Page Fade**: Smooth opacity and slide-in entry keyframes for page loads and modal dialogs.
- **Performance**: Animate compositor-only properties (`transform` and `opacity`).
