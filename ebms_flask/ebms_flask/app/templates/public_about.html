Read more about EBMS

EBMS is Botswana's Electronic Bid Management System. It brings public entities, suppliers, evaluators, and oversight stakeholders into one digital procurement environment, helping each stage move from opportunity publication to submission, evaluation, award, and reporting with greater clarity.
