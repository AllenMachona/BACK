About

Transparent procurement that strengthens public trust.

The Electronic Bid Management System (EBMS) is a digital procurement platform designed to streamline how public entities publish opportunities, manage supplier participation, and coordinate evaluation and award processes. It brings together bidders, procurement teams, and approving officials in one secure environment to improve transparency, accountability, and efficiency.

01

Open access to tender opportunities

Public organisations can publish procurement notices and suppliers can discover current opportunities in a single, consistent portal.

02

Structured supplier participation

Bidders can register, upload required compliance documents, prepare submissions, and track procurement progress with clearer guidance.

03

Governed evaluation and award workflows

Procurement teams can manage reviews, approvals, requests for clarification, and award decisions in a more organised and auditable process.
